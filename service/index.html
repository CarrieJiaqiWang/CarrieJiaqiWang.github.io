Graduate community · August 2024–present

Mentoring and connection at Cornell.

Mathematical exchange

Spaces for sustained discussion.

Spring 2025 · Cornell University

Large deviations reading group

Organized weekly discussions of Dembo and Zeitouni's Large Deviations Techniques and Applications. Coordinated speakers and scheduling and gave three presentations on Cramér's theorem, the Gärtner–Ellis theorem, and the contraction principle.

August 2026 · Banff, Canada

Statistical Inference of Extremes

Hosted this session at Catastrophic Events in the Complex World, introducing speakers, moderating discussion, and supporting session recording.

Outreach

Mathematical discovery beyond the university.

I have led K–12 mathematics and STEM activities through Expanding Your Horizons and the Julia Robinson Mathematics Festival.

During a one-week placement at a rural primary school in China, I taught mathematics and physical education and talked with children about their aspirations. I have also supported robotics competitions.

These experiences strengthened my commitment to making the enjoyment of mathematical discovery accessible to younger learners.

Supporting instructors

Constructive peer feedback.

Through TA consulting, teaching workshops, and peer observation, I help instructors reflect on their goals and identify a specific next step. These conversations also shape my own teaching practice.
