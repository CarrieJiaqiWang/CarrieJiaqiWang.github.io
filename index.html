About

From probability theory
to the structure of extreme episodes.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Operations Research and Information Engineering at Cornell University, advised by Gennady Samorodnitsky. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

My research focuses on large deviations, heavy tails, and dependence. Alongside this theoretical work, I collaborate with Daniel Adrian Maldonado at Argonne National Laboratory on stochastic models of power-grid outages. My earlier work includes differentially private optimization and adaptive derivative-free algorithms.

I hold an M.S. in ORIE from Cornell and a B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.
